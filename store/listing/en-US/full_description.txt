Quill Notes keeps everything you write in one calm place: notes, a daily journal and a task planner. It keeps it private, too. Every note is encrypted on your phone, there is no account to create, and there are no ads.

WRITE
• Format with Markdown: headings, bold, italic, lists and links, with a one-tap toolbar
• Checklists you can tick right from the note card; finished items sink to the bottom
• Add photos from your gallery or camera
• Link notes together with [[double brackets]] and see what links back
• Tags, colours, notebooks, pinning, and an archive for notes you want out of the way
• Templates for meeting notes, weekly reviews and book notes, plus your own
• Dictate by voice, or scan the text in a photo of a page (recognised on your phone)

JOURNAL
• Pick a mood, keep your streak, browse a calendar and see how the month felt
• "On this day" brings back earlier entries
• An optional daily reminder at the time you choose

PLAN
• Tasks with due dates and times, priorities and checklists
• Repeat daily, weekly, monthly or yearly: finishing a task starts the next round
• A week view, a progress ring, and reminders with Done and Snooze buttons

FIND ANYTHING
• One search across notes, journal and tasks, with highlighted matches and filters

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• AES-256-GCM encryption for titles, text, tags and moods, with the key held in the Android Keystore
• Optional biometric lock for the whole app and for individual notes
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking

NEVER LOSE A NOTE
• Optional encrypted backup to your own Google Drive. Notes are encrypted on your phone first, with a recovery passphrase only you know, so you can restore on a new phone and nobody else can read them
• Export everything as Markdown whenever you like, or save one encrypted backup file

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
• Home-screen widgets for quick capture and today's tasks, launcher shortcuts and a Quick Settings tile
• Paper, Midnight and Aurora themes, plus colours from your wallpaper; adjustable text size; tablet and foldable layouts
• English, हिन्दी and मराठी
